How to play
  1. 1
    Turn the phone sideways and hold it on your forehead, screen facing your friends.
  2. 2
    Tilt the phone up when you guess it right (1 point). Tilt it down to pass.
  3. 3
    No tilt sensor? Tap the right half for correct, the left half to pass. On a keyboard use → and ←.
